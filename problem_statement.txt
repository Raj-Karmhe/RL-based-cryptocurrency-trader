1. End-to-End RL based Strategy in Cryptocurrency
markets
(4-6 members)
Background
Alpha is an aspiring software engineer. He wants to get the highest paying job, as he
considers his skills to be top grade (narcissistic)! He learns that a specific company
LameStreet, that creates software for trading in various financial markets around the
world, remains his best choice. But he needs to have a good project to be selected.
He has stumbled upon the following project. Help Alpha to get into LameStreet by
completing this project (in the process, you might become eligible enough to get in
yourself)!
Applying Reinforcement Learning (RL) to financial markets is a notoriously difficult
challenge. While beginner models often focus simply on predicting price direction,
real-world algorithmic trading requires a deep focus on portfolio management, risk
mitigation, and surviving market friction.
A pure predictive model without stop-losses, take-profits, or intelligent capital alloca
tion is effectively gambling. Furthermore, strategies that appear profitable in standard
simulations often collapse in the real world due to transaction costs and execution slip
page. This project requires you to build a complete, robust algorithmic trading pipeline
that treats risk management and position sizing as primary components, rather than
afterthoughts.
Objective
Design, train, and backtest an end-to-end Reinforcement Learning agent to trade a
single cryptocurrency using only historical OHLCV data. The strategy must explicitly
incorporate dynamic position sizing (allocating capital between the asset and cash)
and strict risk management protocols (stop-loss and take-profit mechanisms). The
final agent must be evaluated on a strictly separated out-of-sample test dataset using
a backtesting engine that accounts for real-world execution costs.
Scope of Work
• Data Pipeline:– Acquire approximately 5 years of historical OHLCV (Open, High, Low, Close,
Volume) data for a single cryptocurrency of your choice.– Process the data to make it ready for the RL agent. You are free to do any kind
of data processing you want. You might want to add some features [technical
indicators].

Implementation Note: You may integrate these directly into the RL model’s action

• Backtesting & Evaluation:– Evaluate the final strategy on the 6-month test data, and compile the results into
a csv file.– Following metrics are mandatory to be included in the results- returns, drawdown,
sharpe ratio, win rate.– DO NOT CREATE YOUR OWN BACKTESTER!
• Bonus Extensions:
Make your strategy work for multiple assets. That is, you can create a RL model
that can help you trade on 2 or more cryptocurrencies simultaneously. You can try
changing action space and your position sizer for this. ONLY ATTEMPT THIS
WHEN YOU ARE DONE WITH THE BASE TASK!
Constraints
• External Library Usage: You are free to use any external library or framework
you want for any part of the pipeline (e.g., data acquisition, environment creation,
RL training, or backtesting). However, you must explicitly mention and rigorously
justify the choice of each external library in your final report.
• Data Restriction: You are only allowed to use the historical OHLCV data. No
alternative data, sentiment analysis, or order book depth data may be used.
• Market Friction: The backtesting environment must strictly simulate real-world
constraints, specifically slippage and transaction costs (exchange fees).
• Data Leakage: Absolutely no training or hyperparameter tuning may occur on the
6-month test dataset.
